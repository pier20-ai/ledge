Ledge
nav options — four working simulations · click everything

All four run on the floating-islands chassis: the bar is gone, the beads float beside the notch, the panel hangs clean. Every simulation has three inner views — the app (Focus), the ledge, the chat — and ‹|› always walks apps. What differs is how you reach the ledge and the chat.

Option 1 · The three-way split — ⌂ ✦ ⚙

home shows the ledge · ✦ toggles chat · ⚙ opens the native settings window · chat is just a mode
  • every destination has a truthful door · nothing is hidden
  • three icons on one island — the widest chrome of the four
  • chat de-emphasized: one segment among three

Option 2 · Coverflow chat — neighbors peek

left island is ⌂ ✦ · in chat, the prev/next apps peek at the edges — click a peek (or ‹|›) to switch while staying in chat
  • chat becomes a place you browse apps *from* — paint one, peek the next
  • the peeks double as "where am I in the strip"
  • costs horizontal room in chat · peeks must never steal stage clicks

Option 3 · The standing pill

left island is ⌂ alone · the pill waits at the bottom of every view, dimmed · click it (or type) and the transcript rises · ⌄ drops it
  • the agent is foundationally present — never behind a button
  • one icon of chrome total · costs ~40pt of panel height, always
  • unobtrusive-yet-obvious knob: the pill's resting opacity

Option 4 · The notch is home

no left island at all — click the notch itself for the ledge · the standing pill carries chat · chrome is just ‹|› and the pill
  • the seat is the home button — the most central target on the screen
  • zero left chrome · the quietest of the four
  • risk: nothing *says* the notch is clickable — pure learned magic

My read

I prefer Option 3, with Option 2's stay-in-chat switching folded in (they compose: the standing pill for entry, mode-preserving ‹|› once you're in — the peeks themselves I'd hold until the strip walking feels wrong without them). The pill is the only design where the agent is discovered by existing rather than by being found behind an icon, which is what principle 11 was always claiming. Its height cost is real but honest — every other option hides the product's main idea to save 40 points.

Option 1 is the safest and the most conventional — three truthful doors — but ⚙ pays top-level chrome for something right-click and ⌘, already do, and ✦ still makes the agent a mode you visit. If the pill feels heavy in practice, O1 minus the gear (a ⌂ ✦ split) is my second choice. Option 4 is the one I'd want in a keynote and the one I'd regret shipping first — clickable-notch is a delighter to add later to whichever option wins, not a primary door.
